Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026
HomeTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

This policy covers "Video Uploader", a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Stubby Pencil channel to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.

Data the tool handles

  • TikTok sign-in tokens (access token, refresh token, open ID) for the owner's own account, received through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • The owner's own video files, sent to TikTok's upload endpoint.

How it is stored

Tokens are kept only in a local file on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's user account. They are not sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

What the tool does not do

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person, TikTok user or viewer. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no server of its own.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.
