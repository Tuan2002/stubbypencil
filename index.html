Stubby Pencil makes short, wordless cartoons in a pencil-and-crayon style about everyday struggles: a mosquito at 3 AM, laundry and sudden rain, waiting for instant noodles.

Watch on YouTube and TikTok.

Video Uploader

"Video Uploader" is a small private desktop tool used only by the channel owner. It sends the channel's own finished videos to the owner's TikTok drafts (TikTok Content Posting API, "upload to inbox"), where the owner reviews, captions and posts them in the TikTok app. It is not offered to other users.
